Why play Black Mage?

Black Mage is a caster that focuses on high personal damage output while foregoing specialized raid utility and a non-distinct burst window that has to be aligned with other jobs. This allows the individual player to focus on playing to the best of their abilities while not being punished for misaligning cooldowns. Black Mage also has the benefit of having a very low off-global cooldown (oGCD/Abilities) that makes it much lower in actions per minute (APM) than other jobs.
