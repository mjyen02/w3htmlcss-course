Guide Overview / Job Basics

As stated in the home page, Black Mage focuses on looping a core rotation to steadily put out damage as opposed to favoring a burst window that many other jobs build up towards. This guide is going to likely parrot a lot of what the Balance will say (and I strong recommend reading their guide for more profesionalism).

Job Mechanics

Black Mage's core rotation is swapping between the Astral Fire (AF) and Umbral Ice (UI) stances. We can consider Astral Fire to be the "damage stance" while Umbral Ice is the "recovery stance." During Astral Fire, fire-aspected spells (Fire I, Fire II, Fire III, and Fire IV) gain increased potency while doubling the MP cost of said spells. Meanwhile, during Umbral Ice, ice-aspected spells become free to cast and will regenerate MP in proportion to the level of Umbral Ice present.

Astral Fire Bonuses
  1. Astral Fire 1 (AF1): Fire Spells do 40% Extra Damage
  2. Astral Fire 2 (AF2): Fire Spells do 60% Extra Damage
  3. Astral Fire 3 (AF3): Fire Spells do 80% Extra Damage
    • Secondary Effect: Ice Spell casts are halved and are free.
Umbral Ice Bonuses
  1. Umbral Ice 1 (UI1): Ice Spells regenerate 2500 MP
  2. Umbral Ice 2 (UI2): Ice Spells regenerate 5000 MP
  3. Umbral Ice 3 (UI3): Ice Spells regenerate 10000 MP
    • Secondary Effect: Fire Spell casts are halved and are free.

Unaspected Spells like Foul, Xenoglossy, Paradox, and Scathe (lol) are not modified by Astral Fire or Umbral Ice due to not being aspected with either element.

Enochian is a passive damage bonus gained while maintaining any level of stance (AF1-3 or UI1-3) will preserve the bonus damage. It will only be lost upon fully dropping a stance which would require dying or accidentally casting the opposite spell's first level (Fire 1 or Blizzard 1).

Umbral Hearts are a resource generated by casting Blizzard 4 or Freeze (Level 58+) or Umbral Soul. They negate the doubled MP cost of Astral Fire spells until no stacks are present. Any number of Umbral Hearts will reduce Flare's MP Cost by two-thirds. Notably, Despair is not affected by Umbral Hearts.

Polyglot is gained from maintaining Enochian for thirty seconds. At level 70+, one stack is permitted, at 80+, two stacks are permitted, and at 98+ up to three. The timer completing when full on stacks will cause the newly generated polyglot to be lost, and the timer will reset-- so be careful to not overcap.

Paradox is generated from switching from Astral Fire 3 to Umbral Ice (any level) OR switching from Umbral Ice 3 AND having three umbral heart stacks to Astral Fire 3. Paradox behaves different between the two stances and replaces Blizzard 1 and Fire 1 . In Umbral Ice, it is simply an instant cast and has no MP cost. In Astral Fire, it will generate a firestarter proc (Instant and free Fire 3). The job gauge will display a visual element when Paradox is castable.

Last but not least is Astral Soul. One stack of Astral Soul is generated for every Fire IV casted during an Astral Fire cycle. It is denoted by the revolver-shell appearing job gauge element. At six stacks, you will be able to cast Flarestar which at level 100 is Black Mage's most powerful spell. Be wary that leaving Astral Fire will cause you to lose your currently built Astral Soul stacks.

The Job Gauge (Elemental Compass)

  1. Astral Fire and Umbral Ice Stacks
  2. Umbral Heart Stacks
  3. Polyglot Gauge
  4. Polyglot Timer
  5. Paradox Marker
  6. Astral Soul Gauge

General Job Gameplay

Black Mage's gameplay will generally look like:

  • Blizzard 3 (B3)
  • Blizzard 4 (B4)
  • Umbral Ice Paradox
  • Fire 3 (F3)
  • Six Fire 4's (F4)
  • Astral Fire Paradox
  • Despair
  • Flarestar
The general rotation has you casting these spells while maintaining your Thunder Damage Over Time (DoT) spell throughout the rotation. Astral Fire Paradox can generally be slotted whenever needed to weave off-globals during Astral Fire or if one GCD of movement is required at any point. Casting six Fire IV is important as it allows you to end your Astral Fire phase with both Despair and Flarestar.

In Umbral Ice, Blizzard 4 is required to generate both the Astral Fire Paradox and regain the required MP to begin another Astral Fire cycle. The Paradox gained in Umbral Ice should also be used before swapping. Do not forget to spent Polyglots on Xenoglossy (single target) or Foul (2+ Targets) before overcapping.

The Opener

This opener skips a Despair pre-Manafont so that an extra Flarestar in the opener can be generated. It is called the 5+7 opener due to the number of casts of Fire IV present on either half manafont. Some additional personal suggestions I have are:

  • The second charge of Leylines can be used whenever in the opener should you want an extra cast under pot. That said, if you cannot get a full use of Leylines (forced exit at some point) then save the extra charge for whenever it is convenient.
  • Triplecast and Swiftcast are flexible throughout the opener, but should ideally still only be used after instant spells.
  • Notably, this opener does not generate a Firestarter proc due to using all MP on Fire IV until the second use of manafont in the fight. While the balance will recommend to use their alternative opener. I believe it is fine to just hardcast and incur the damage penalty on Blizzard 3 while still learning the job. See more below where I talk about rotational optimizations that can be made.

Single Target Rotation

Here is a picture form of the standard single target rotation. As described in the general gameplay section, you are aiming to cast six Fire IV during Astral Fire to generate a Flarestar. Some important things to note:

  1. They use transpose and swiftcast or triplecast on the Blizzard 3, for beginners, this is not necessary. Focus on uptime.
  2. I strongly recommend always trying to cast Despair after Flarestar to build better habits when building up to the above optimization.
  3. As I previously stated too, Astral Fire's Paradox is flexible and is fine anywhere within the cycle, just do not forget to use it.
  4. You may have noticed there is a transpose following Umbral Ice Paradox. I recommend learning this once you hit level 90+ as there is a significant gain in doing so the entire fight. The reason this is done is because you skip the 30% damage penalty incurred by casting a fire spell from Umbral Ice. And it benefits from the Astral Fire 1 40% damage bonus.

High Thunder (DoT)

In Dawntrail, you gain one use of Thunder (via the thunderhead proc) every time you swap stances. The general idea is to refresh your DoT whenever it has three or less seconds due to how FFXIV handles DoTs. They activate every three seconds on the server tick. The DoT is going to drift throughout your rotation as one full cycle of your rotation is longer than thirty seconds, so just do your best to keep it up. It is also valuable as a weave window during your natural refreshes.

Polyglot (Xenoglossy/Foul)

Your polyglot spells are powerful spells that serve both as high damage, movement tools, and weaving tools. Use these for movement as needed, but it is beneficial to fit any unneeded stacks during the raid buffs.

AoE Rotation (3+)

With Fire 2 and Blizzard 2 being very weak in potency, it is worth skipping them. I would not ordinarily suggest using transpose lines to beginners, but it is very much worth practicing the preceeding rotation displayed in aoe. Instant casts (Foul) at lower levels can be substituted with just casting more Freezes or using thunder early at the end of Astral Fire.

2-Target

The same rotation is applicable to two target, however, just replace Freeze with Blizzard 4

Off-Global Cooldowns (oGCDs)

As a generalization, using an off-global after a hardcast will result in "clipping (delaying)" your next GCD due to the animation lock associated with using abilities (0.7s). In emergencies, it is always better to use Addle, Manaward, or Aetherial Manipulation to try and salvage a situation. Now for advice for Black Mage's major cooldowns.

Leylines

Standing inside of leylines will grant you the Circle of Power buff which will reduce both the cast times and recast times (GCD speed) by 15%. Leylines will generally be used off cooldown, though it is worth holding them if you know it will be difficult to utilize the full duration at given periods of time throughout fights. The second leyline charge should be used whenever the earliest window to utilize its full duration is possible. Some smaller additional niche tips:

  • Standing at one edge of Leylines is typically a good practice as not all AoEs will cover your leyline from one edge to the other. This may allow you to not have to use retrace to reset your leyline positioning.
  • If the above does not work, but leaves space next to your leyline, you can take advantage of buff snapshotting and use an instant cast spell to quickly exit and re-enter your leylines before your next GCD is available. This will still give you the full benefit of the leyline speed bonus while not forcing you to remain within the circle the entire time.
  • If all else fails, use retrace at a spot you know is likely to be more space and utilize the above tips to try to maximize your Circle of Power uptime.

Triplecast and Swiftcast

These should be used primarily to facilitate your movement needs. Be mindful that spells that are already instant like Xenglossy, Paradox, and Thunder will not consume the buff. The secondary consideration for these two abilities will be to wield another off-global down the line such as if you need to chain two triplecasts back to back.

Manafont

Manafont should be used anytime it is available and you have completed a fire phase. There is no going over the MP cap of 10,000 so try to be efficient with it's use. Delaying manafont is fine as it doesn't directly increase your damage within a small burst window, just try not to let it drift for more than one cycle.

Rest of your oGCDs

  • Amplified should be used off cooldown to gain more stacks of polyglot throughout the fight.
  • Aetherial Manipulation should be your movement tool for covering large distances within one GCD as your teammates will likely position for you.
  • Manaward is your personal defensive. Use it when you think you will not be nearby healers for some reason.
  • Transpose will primarily be used at higher levels of gameplay to facilitate optimizing your damage on lack Mage.
  • Addle is your party mitigation, use it on the target before raidwide damage to help your team.
  • Surecast is used to negate knockback effects. It also allows your casts to be uninterrupted should you be auto'd or take avoidable damage for some reason.
  • Lucid Dreaming recovers MP, but only works in Umbral Ice, therefore outside of high end optimization this will generally be a useless ability.

Handling Downtime and Recovering from Mistakes

Making mistakes is inevitable when learning a fight so it is good to know some recovery options. Should you die or accidentally drop your stance via casting a tier 1 spell of the opposite element, it is typically worth just restarting from Umbral Ice 3, with swift or triplecast if possible because of it's unmodified 3.5s cast time.

Ending a fight or preparing for Downtime

If a boss is soon to die or become untargettable consider the following:

  • DoTs do not tick when the boss is not targettable or if it does, if you believe the boss to be immune or die within the next 24 seconds, skip your DoT refresh.
  • This one will require practice and experience in the fight itself, but just make sure you try to avoid overcapping your polyglot during downtime when the boss is not targettable. Xenoglossy is your second strongest spell.
  • Speaking of Xenoglossy, try to prioritize stronger spells before downtime. As an example, if you have to cast Despair or Flarestar, prioritize Flarestar for it's 500 potency versus Despair's 340. Or as another example, use Paradox over Blizzard IV in Umbral Ice.
  • One last more niche tip. Due to cast times making your spell's damage come out later, using an instant spell just before a boss becomes immune may lead to you being able to squeeze out just a little more damage.

Continuing Post Downtime

  • With the removal of the Astral Fire and Umbral Ice timers, it is typically okay for short downtime (around 10s) to just continue your rotation where you left off. Just try to remember not to waste thunderhead prior to downtime.
  • If a downtime is longer, consider ending your Astral Fire cycle early before downtime using Despair. Then regenerate MP during downtime via transpose and Umbral Soul.
  • As an extension of the previous point, if downtime is incredibly long, it is possible to generate another thunder and paradox through downtime. To achieve this- end your astral fire phase as previously discussed. Then, use Umbral Soul three times to get to Umbral Ice 3 with three Umbral hearts. Use transpose two more times then utilize Umbral Soul another 3 times to get back to max. This will refully reset your resources barring polyglot.

Optimizations to Make

Assuming you are comfortable with the basics of Black Mage, it is time to work on optimizing damage. As previously shown in the single target rotation, it is possible to make your Blizzard 3 instant via Swiftcast or Triplecase . This is a damage gain if you perform it alongside transpose to skip the previously discussed opposite element penalty. Additionally, there is a different rotation to utilize until your first Manafont should you correctly make Blizzard 3 Instant in the opener.
