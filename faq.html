What Spell Speed Should I Play?

I have no personal recommendations for spell speed. Having played both fast and slow, I personally like slow more, but fast spell speed also feels cool in its own way. I will recommend three tiers of speed however.

  • Fast: 2.17-2.20
  • Medium: 2.32-2.36
  • Slow: 2.42 or higher

What Should I use Instants On?

  • Triplecast: Largely for movement, use on Instant B3 when not needed
  • Swiftcast: Same as Triple
  • Xenoglossy: Weave oGCDs, movement, or to not overcap on polyglot

I'm having Trouble with Movement in a Fight

This is going to be a little more vague, but try to be cleaner in your movements. For example, try to move in a straight line where you need to go. If this doesn't resolve some minor movement issues, try to apply it to earlier in the fight as well. If you are spending polyglot in burst, save them for when you need movement.
